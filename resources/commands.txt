!sr [name] - add song to queue (everyone)
!oops - remove last song request from queue by the same user (everyone)
!queue - show queue (MOD, VIP)
!next - switch to the next song (MOD, VIP)
!clear - clear queue (Streamer)
!remove [index] - remove song from queue at the specified index (MOD)
